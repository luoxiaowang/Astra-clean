# Astra 行星纹理署名

纹理取自本机 Mole 1.17.0 资源包，保留其第三方来源及改动声明。Astra 重新编写球体渲染与动画，不复制 Mole 可执行代码。

## Solar System Scope textures

Mole includes the official Mercury, Mars, Jupiter, Sun, and Moon textures
from the Solar System Scope texture library.

- **Source:** <https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/>
- **Copyright:** Copyright (c) 2010-2017, Solar System Scope
- **License:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
- **License text:** <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>

**Changes made for Mole:** `mercury.png` and `mars.png` were resampled as square
maps for Mole's spherical renderer. `mars.png` was also color-adjusted.
`moon.jpg` was tone-adjusted: a pale cream tint and deeper maria. The other
textures are the official downloads without changes.

**Bundled files:** `mercury.png`, `mars.png`, `jupiter.jpg`, `sun.jpg`, and
`moon.jpg`.

## NASA Visible Earth imagery

Mole's Earth texture combines land and cloud imagery from NASA's Blue Marble
collection.

- **Land source:**
  <https://visibleearth.nasa.gov/images/57752/blue-marble-land-surface-shallow-water-and-shaded-topography>
- **Cloud source:**
  <https://visibleearth.nasa.gov/images/57747/blue-marble-clouds/77558l>
- **Credit:** NASA Goddard Space Flight Center. Image by Reto Stöckli, with
  enhancements by Robert Simmon.
- **Usage guidelines:**
  <https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/>

NASA states that its content is generally not subject to copyright in the
United States. Use remains subject to NASA's media guidelines, including source
acknowledgement and no implied endorsement.

**Changes made for Mole:** the land image was resized to 4096 x 2048, with
adjustments to land brightness and ocean color. The cloud map was gamma-adjusted
and screen-blended over the land image.

**Bundled file:** `earth.jpg`.
